## Working Papers & Work in Progress

* **How Much Sustainability is Really in Stock Prices?** (with E. Lütkebohmert, M. Pelger e M. Rockel) **[Job Market Paper]**
> We find no robust evidence that sustainability information predicts stock returns. The results are twofold. First, machine learning applied to 22 environmental characteristics from four major data providers predicts neither returns ($t = 1.08$) nor the component of returns orthogonal to 45 conventional firm characteristics ($t = 0.84$). Notably, the same model applied to conventional characteristics delivers large and significant out-of-sample performance ($\text{SR} = 0.58$, $t = 2.47$). Second, through replicating prominent evidence of green outperformance, we show that its significance is an artifact of research design: the sample period, the investment universe, the portfolio granularity, the prediction target, and the absence of multiple-testing corrections. No specification survives standard corrections for data snooping.
