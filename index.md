## About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Quantitative Finance in the [Department of Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University, advised by [Markus Pelger](https://mpelger.people.stanford.edu/), and a member of the [Advanced Technologies Lab](https://fintech.stanford.edu). In Summer 2025, I was a Quantitative Algorithmic Trading intern at Bank of America in NYC. Previously, I graduated summa cum laude with an MS in Data Science from [Bocconi University](https://www.unibocconi.it/en), advised by [Carlo Favero](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/faculty/carlo-ambrogio-favero).

In May 2026 I represented Italy in foil and sabre at the [Para Fencing World Cup](https://iwasfencing.sports.organchest.com/) in Bradenton, FL.

Dissertation committee: [Markus Pelger](https://mpelger.people.stanford.edu/), [Kay Gieseke](https://profiles.stanford.edu/kay-gieseke), and [Jose Blanchet](https://profiles.stanford.edu/jose-blanchet).

**I am on the 2026-27 academic job market.**
