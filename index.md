I am a PhD Candidate in Quantitative Finance in the [Department of Management Science and Engineering](https://msande.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University, advised by [Markus Pelger](https://mpelger.people.stanford.edu/). I am also a member of the [Advanced Technologies Lab](https://fintech.stanford.edu) at Stanford. In the Summer 2025, I was an intern in the Quantitative Algorithmic Trading team of Bank of America in NYC. Before Stanford, I graduated summa cum laude with a MS in Data Science from Bocconi University, where I was advised by [Carlo Favero](https://www.unibocconi.it/en/faculty/carlo-ambrogio-favero). 

In my free time I play wheelchair fencing and in May 2026 I represented Italy at the [Para Fencing World Cup](/assets/img/wc.jpg) in Bradenton, Florida.

Dissertation committee: [Markus Pelger](https://mpelger.people.stanford.edu/), [Kay Giesecke](https://giesecke.people.stanford.edu), and [Jose Blanchet](https://giesecke.people.stanford.edu).
